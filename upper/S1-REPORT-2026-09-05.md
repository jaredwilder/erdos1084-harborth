## 2. THE MATHEMATICS — S1 IS MOSTLY FREE, AND THE RESIDUE IS ONE SIGN

The frozen S1 asked for two cross-product **products**; the sealed S2 additionally needs the
orientation `hor : 0 < cross Y X Z`. Writing `u, m, w` for the vectors from `v₀` to
`p i, p (i+1), p (i+2)`:

```
cross (p(i+1)) (p i)     v₀ = −(u × m) = + r_i   r_{i+1} sin(a_i     − a_{i+1})
cross (p(i+1)) (p (i+2)) v₀ = + (m × w) = − r_{i+1} r_{i+2} sin(a_{i+1} − a_{i+2})
cross (p(i+1)) (p i) (p (i+2)) = (u × w) − (u × m) − (m × w)
```

**Two of the three signs are FREE from the polar data alone** — descending arguments with
gaps in `(0, π)` make both sines positive. Only the orientation carries geometry, and even
it is free on one side: when the two consecutive gaps sum to **more** than `π`, `u × w > 0`
and the whole expression is a sum of three positives. **Convexity is needed in exactly one
regime: `u × w < 0`, i.e. a salient cone.**

There, **Cramer** does the work. `m = λu + μw` with

```
λ = (m × w)/(u × w) > 0        μ = (u × m)/(u × w) > 0        (both quotients of negatives)
hor = (u × w)·(1 − λ − μ)
```

so `hor ≤ 0` forces `λ + μ ≤ 1`, and then `p (i+1)` is a convex combination of `v₀`, `p i`,
`p (i+2)` with **strictly positive** weight on the outer two. Split on `λ + μ`: below `1` the
point sits in `openSegment v₀ c`, at `1` it sits in `openSegment (p i) (p (i+2))` — either
way an **extreme point of `A` is in an open segment of two points of `A`**, which
`mem_extremePoints` forbids. Contradiction; `hor > 0`.

Both non-degeneracies the argument needs (`p (i+1) ≠ v₀`, `p (i+1) ≠ p i`) fall out of the
single fact `u × m < 0`, not from extra hypotheses.

⛔ **Note what this proof did NOT need:** no supporting hyperplane, no `frontier`, no
`geometric_hahn_banach_open_point`, no interior. The recon map's route (HULL-ANGLE-API-MAP
headline 3) was the expensive one. Extremality + Cramer is four screens of Lean.

## 3. THREE STATEMENT DEFECTS IN THE FROZEN S1 — AND WHY THEY MATTER

`H3_Decomposition_SCAFFOLD.lean` froze S1 before S2 was proved. Then S2 was sealed with an
extra hypothesis, and the socket no longer fits. All three are repaired in `H3S1`.

**(A) ORIENTATION — `hnn : 0 ≤ a (i+1) − a i` is the wrong direction.** Ascending arguments
is the COUNTERCLOCKWISE listing; `InsideAngleAt` selects the CLOCKWISE one. This is the
*same* defect `O06_PolarFan_Handoff_Desc.lean` found in the O06 socket one rung earlier —
it had already been repaired on the consumer side (`o06_angle_sum_of_polar_fan_desc`,
descending) and **the producer's copy was never updated**. Kernel-checked as
`ascending_defeats_hor`: under ascending strict gaps, `cross (p(i+1)) (p i) v₀ < 0` at every
index, so the frozen conclusion FORCES `cross (p(i+1)) (p i) (p(i+2)) < 0` — the exact
negation of S2's `hor`. **The ascending S1 is not merely unproved: proved in full it could
never be consumed.**

**(B) THE CONCLUSION IS INCOMPLETE — `hor` is missing.** The frozen S1 concluded only the two
products; both are **orientation-invariant** (reversing the listing swaps the clauses), so
they cannot possibly deliver the orientation S2 needs. The repaired S1 emits all three signs.

**(C) `frontier` IS NOT ENOUGH — the listing must be EXTREME points.** With three collinear
listed points every cross product vanishes and both `0 < _ * _` clauses are FALSE. A point of
`S` in the relative interior of a hull edge is on the frontier and is not extreme, so the
frozen `hmem` admits exactly that. Kernel-checked as `collinear_kills_all_clauses`. This
matches the campaign's own probe evidence (`h3probe4`: hull vertices only, 30,000
configurations, **zero** failures).

## 5. WHAT REMAINS — AT ITS TRUE STRENGTH

⛔ **`H3X` IS NOT `FanInterface`, AND MUST NOT BE FILED AS ONE.** `H05_Assembly.lean`'s
`FanInterface` indexes `hullPts S = {v ∈ S | v ∈ frontier (convexHull ℝ S)}` and demands
**surjectivity onto it**. `H3X` indexes EXTREME points. The two sets genuinely differ when
`S` has three collinear boundary points, and Mathlib has no bridge between them — its own
TODO (`Extreme.lean:40`, `Exposed.lean:41`), recorded as Risk 1 in the API map. Two things
are open:

1. **`vertexPts ⊆ hullPts`** — an extreme point is not interior (route: the separating
   functional; the API map calls this the cheap direction).
2. **The non-extreme frontier points.** They contribute interior angle exactly `π`, so
   `(h−2)π` stays true over all frontier points — but `H3X` does not prove it, because its
   own route (strict cross products) is *false* at a collinear triple. A separate degenerate
   branch is needed. **This is the gap between `H3X` and `H05`'s fan branch.**

⛔ **THE STRICT GAP CEILING IS A HYPOTHESIS, NOT A THEOREM.** `hlt : ∀ i, a i − a (i+1) < π`
is assumed. It IS forced by `v₀` being interior — a gap of exactly `π` puts `v₀` on a chord
with every other listed point in one closed half-plane, so that line supports the hull and
`v₀` lands on the frontier — but **that derivation is not kernel-proved here and is DECLARED
UNPROVED**. `O02_GapCeiling.lean` derives the ceiling `≤ π` from the O01 floor at `m ≥ 4`
neighbours; the strict `<` at the hull layer is a different obligation.

⛔ **DEFECT (C)'s EXISTENTIAL HALF IS UNPROVED.** `collinear_kills_all_clauses` is a *local*
fact about a collinear triple. That a finite `S` exists whose frozen hull hypotheses ALL hold
with a collinear listed triple is supported only by the Python probes, not by the kernel.

**Still open above this rung, unchanged:** the per-vertex cone-packing bound feeding
`FanInterface`'s last clause (H04's `h4_cone_packing` is sealed; its instantiation is not),
`hcentral` at `m ≤ 3` (Contract C §3), and the construction of the sorted listing itself
(Risk 3: θ-injectivity on hull points from an interior point).
